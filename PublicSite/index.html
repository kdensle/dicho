Why dicho?

Most translators give you generic Spanish. dicho adapts phrasing to the country you choose — Mexico, Spain, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, Peru, Puerto Rico, or U.S. Spanish. The result sounds like something a real person would actually say.

Features

  • Automatic English and Spanish detection
  • Country-aware Spanish for 8 regions
  • Natural English translation from Spanish
  • Auto-copy output for quick pasting
  • Light, dark, and system appearance modes
  • 30 free translations per month
  • Optional dicho pro subscription for unlimited access

Built for real messages

dicho is designed for private messages, quick replies, and tone-sensitive everyday communication. Enter a message, pick a country, and get a translation you can paste right into your conversation.

Translations are AI-generated and may be imperfect. Always review important messages before sending.
